About this guide

Most of us spend very little time thinking about what our clothes are made from — yet the fabrics we wear sit against our skin all day, and the chemicals used to produce, treat, and finish textiles can matter for health and environmental reasons.

This guide cuts through the noise to give you practical, sourced information about common fabric types, what to look for, and where the evidence is strongest.

📋 Note: This guide is general information only. Not medical or dietary advice. Always consider your own circumstances when making purchasing decisions.
