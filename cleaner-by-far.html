Lighten Your Load · Australian Produce Guide

✅ Fill your basket

Produce items with the lowest evidence of pesticide risk in the Australian context. Conventional versions are generally a reasonable choice - saving your organic budget for the Dirty Dozen.

✅ More fresh produce is always better than less! These carry lower residues than most, so fill your basket, and spend your organic dollars where they count most.
  • 1🌿 AsparagusCleanestConsistently on the EWG Clean Fifteen — fewer than 11% of samples globally show any detectable residues. Short growing season limits spray applications, and thick stalks offer natural protection. Buy AU-grown in season (Sept–Feb) where available.
  • 2🥑 AvocadosCleanestThick inedible skin. Less than 2% of samples globally show any residues. Consistently the cleanest item on any produce list.
  • 3🍌 BananasSafePeeled before eating — flesh well protected. 2026 EWG Clean Fifteen. Note: conventional banana skins carry post-harvest fungicide residues — avoid feeding peels to wildlife.
  • 4🟣 BeetrootSafeUnderground crop, thick skin almost always peeled. Low pest pressure. Low residues internationally.
  • 5🥬 CabbageSafeOuter leaves discarded. Produces glucosinolates that naturally deter insects, reducing how much spraying is needed.
  • 6🥕 CarrotsSafeUnderground crop, skin discarded. On 2026 EWG Clean Fifteen. Low residue data in AU and globally.
  • 7⚪ CauliflowerSafeDense tightly packed head, outer leaves discarded. Low residues in all available data. Predominantly AU-grown.
  • 8🍆 EggplantSafeThick skin, lower pest pressure than thin-skinned crops. Low detection rates. Predominantly AU-grown.
  • 9🫛 Frozen garden peas (shelled)SafeProtective pod discarded before freezing. Very low residues. Distinct from snow peas — the whole pod is not eaten.
  • 10🥝 KiwifruitSafeSkin not consumed. 82% of samples globally had zero detectable residues in the flesh.
  • 11🌿 LeeksSafeAllium family — outer layers discarded. Low pest pressure. Predominantly AU-grown. Consistent with onion and shallot residue profile.
  • 12🥭 MangoesSafeThick inedible skin, low residues. Buy local QLD/NT where possible — interstate transit treatment applies to some mangoes.
  • 13🍄 MushroomsSafeGrown in controlled indoor environments in AU. Low pesticide use. Generally low residues in available data.
  • 14🧅 OnionsSafePapery outer skin. Low pest pressure. Very low residues in AU data. Allium family consistently clean.
  • 15🥭 Papaya / PawpawSafeThick skin, flesh well-protected. Low detection rates. Both AU-grown and imported perform well globally.
  • 16🍍 PineapplesSafeVery thick inedible skin — near-zero residue detections globally. Mainly imported but flesh is well protected.
  • 17🎃 PumpkinSafeThick inedible skin. Low pest pressure. Flesh consistently clean globally. Predominantly AU-grown.
  • 18🌱 Shallots & spring onionsSafeAllium family — papery layers, low pest pressure. Short-cycle crop, predominantly AU-grown. Low residues.
  • 19🌽 Sweet cornSafeHusk protection. Very low residues. AU sweet corn is non-GMO unlike many US equivalents.
  • 20🍠 Sweet potatoSafeUnderground crop, skin usually peeled. Low residue rates. A common AU kitchen staple with a reassuring profile.
  • 21🍉 WatermelonSafeVery thick rind. Flesh well insulated from chemicals. Low residues across all global studies consistently.

✅ General Practices

  • Check country of origin - AU-grown Dirty Dozen items are preferable to imported equivalents
  • Soak in baking soda solution (1 tsp per 2 cups water, 15 minutes) then rinse - reduces surface residues more effectively than water alone
  • Farmers markets - ask about spray practices; many small AU farms use organic methods without formal certification
  • Grow your own - focus effort on Dirty Dozen crops: strawberries, leafy greens, kale, and berries
