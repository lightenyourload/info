Lighten Your Load · Privacy

Your privacy

This site is about knowing what you're taking in. It's only fair to be just as clear about what the site takes in from you.

In short: We count visits and downloads so we know which guides are useful. We ask for your email when you download a guide, and we'll only use it to send occasional Lighten Your Load updates. We will never sell or share it. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

Counting visits (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: which pages people read, how they found the site, roughly where in the world they are, and how often each guide is downloaded. This tells us whether the work is reaching people and which guides to improve next.

Google Analytics uses cookies to tell new visits from returning ones. We don't see who you are, and we don't use the data for advertising. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy.

If you'd rather not be counted, you can block cookies for this site in your browser, use a content blocker, or install Google's opt-out add-on. The guides work exactly the same either way.

Email updates

When you download a guide or sign up for updates, we store your email address and the date you signed up. If you choose to answer the optional questions, we also store whether you’d heard of the Dirty Dozen and your postcode. We use these only to understand who the guides are reaching and to connect people locally, never to identify you to anyone else. For now everything is kept in a private Google Sheet that isn't shared with anyone else, collected through Google Forms.

  • We'll only email you about Lighten Your Load: when a guide is updated with new data, or a new guide is published. Expect a few emails a year, not a few a week.
  • We will never sell, rent or share your email address with anyone.
  • Every email will include a way to unsubscribe. You can also ask to be removed at any time by emailing lightenyourloadinfo@proton.me.
  • If we move the list to a dedicated email newsletter service, we'll say so in the first email sent from it, so you can unsubscribe straight away if you'd prefer.

Contributions

Contributions are processed by Stripe and received through Lighten Your Life, the registered business name this project runs under. You’ll see “Lighten Your Load” (or “LightenYL”) on your card statement. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never pass through this site. We receive your name, email address and the amount, so we can keep records and say thank you. We don't use those details for anything else, and contributing doesn't sign you up for emails.

Downloads

Downloading a guide is free. We ask for your email first, so we can let you know when new data changes a guide - your postcode and the Dirty Dozen question are optional. We count downloads (which guide) and chip-in clicks (the amount chosen) through Google Analytics, without linking them to you.

Behind the scenes

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like most web services, they may briefly record technical details such as your IP address to deliver the pages. We don't have access to those records.

Questions or requests

To see what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted, email lightenyourloadinfo@proton.me. We'll respond as soon as we can.

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date below.
