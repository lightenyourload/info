Lighten Your Load · Privacy

Your privacy

This site is about knowing what you're taking in. It's only fair to be just as clear about what the site takes in from you.

In short: I count visits and downloads so I know which guides are useful. If you give me your email, I'll only use it to send occasional Lighten Your Load updates. I will never sell or share it. You can ask me to remove it at any time.

Counting visits (Google Analytics)

I use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: which pages people read, how they found the site, roughly where in the world they are, and how often each guide is downloaded. This tells me whether the work is reaching people and which guides to improve next.

Google Analytics uses cookies to tell new visits from returning ones. I don't see who you are, and I don't use the data for advertising. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy.

If you'd rather not be counted, you can block cookies for this site in your browser, use a content blocker, or install Google's opt-out add-on. The guides work exactly the same either way.

Email updates

If you sign up for updates, I store your email address and the date you signed up. For now these are kept in a private Google Sheet that only I can access, collected through Google Forms.

  • I'll only email you about Lighten Your Load: when a guide is updated with new data, or a new guide is published. Expect a few emails a year, not a few a week.
  • I will never sell, rent or share your email address with anyone.
  • Every email will include a way to unsubscribe. You can also ask to be removed at any time by emailing lightenyourloadinfo@proton.me.
  • If I move the list to a dedicated email newsletter service, I'll say so in the first email sent from it, so you can unsubscribe straight away if you'd prefer.

Contributions

Contributions are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never pass through this site. I receive your name, email address and the amount, so I can keep records and say thank you. I don't use those details for anything else, and contributing doesn't sign you up for emails.

Downloads

Downloading a guide is free and doesn't require your email or any other details. I count downloads (which guide, and the amount you chose, including $0) through Google Analytics, without linking them to you.

Behind the scenes

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like most web services, they may briefly record technical details such as your IP address to deliver the pages. I don't have access to those records.

Questions or requests

To see what I hold about you, correct it or have it deleted, email lightenyourloadinfo@proton.me. I'll respond as soon as I can.

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date below.
