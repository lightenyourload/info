📚 Resources

The data behind these guides comes from Australian government monitoring, independent research, and advocacy organisations. Here's what I used and where to go next.

🌿

Pesticide Action Australia

The peak body advocating for the reduction of pesticide use in Australia. PAA monitors government policy, supports independent research, and campaigns for safer food systems. Their 2026 report with independent testing has helped me immensely in confirming the data in these guides.

Data Sources

FSANZ Australian Total Diet Study (ATDS)
Food Standards Australia New Zealand · Government monitoring
The primary Australian government program that measures dietary exposure to pesticide residues. Analyses a market basket of commonly eaten foods to estimate actual consumer exposure. Used as the baseline for Australian-specific residue data across all three guides.

Friends of the Earth Australia — Pesticide Residues in Australian Food (2012)
Friends of the Earth AU · Independent research
A landmark independent analysis of 12 years of FSANZ ATDS data (2000–2011) identifying which produce items carried the highest detection rates and chemical loads in Australia. One of the few systematic AU-specific rankings available and a foundational source for the Dirty Dozen and Tread Careful Ten.

CHOICE Australia
CHOICE · Consumer advocacy
Australia's leading independent consumer organisation. CHOICE has conducted and published investigations into pesticide residues in Australian-bought produce, including testing of imported garlic and other high-risk items. Used to supplement government data with independent consumer-focused findings.

National Residue Survey (NRS)
Australian Department of Agriculture · Government monitoring
Government program monitoring chemical residues in Australian agricultural produce, primarily for export compliance purposes. Provides data on what AU-grown produce carries at the farm gate, complementing the ATDS market basket data.

APVMA — Australian Pesticides and Veterinary Medicines Authority
Federal regulator · Approval & review data
The federal body responsible for approving and reviewing agricultural chemicals in Australia. APVMA's proposed and completed suspension decisions — such as the 2025 dimethoate review affecting blueberries, raspberries and blackberries — are cited directly in the Dirty Dozen guide.
Dirty DozenTread Careful TenLighter Load
EWG Shopper's Guide to Pesticides in Produce (2026)
Environmental Working Group (US) · Annual report
The US Environmental Working Group's annual analysis of USDA pesticide testing data, producing the widely-cited Dirty Dozen and Clean Fifteen lists. Used in these guides as international comparative data and specifically for chemicals such as PFAS (bifenthrin, fludioxonil) where AU-specific data is absent. US rankings are not directly applicable to Australia but provide relevant context where AU data gaps exist.
Dirty DozenTread Careful TenLighter Load
The Guardian Australia — FOI investigation
The Guardian AU · Investigative journalism · FOI documents
Freedom of Information documents obtained by The Guardian Australia revealing suppressed government pesticide testing data, including detections of fenthion at unacceptable levels in peaches and dimethoate up to 90× the MRL in strawberries (2013 data). This reporting uncovered data the government had not proactively disclosed.

Southern Cross University — Dimethoate research (2025)
Southern Cross University · Academic research · Dirty Dozen specific
2025 research from Southern Cross University finding dimethoate residues in Australian blueberries at levels exceeding safe daily intake estimates. A key piece of AU-specific evidence cited in the Dirty Dozen blueberry entry and context for the APVMA suspension proposal.
Dirty Dozen
Hort Innovation — Residue and production data (2025)
Hort Innovation · Industry body · Tread Careful Ten specific
Australia's horticulture industry research and development body. Hort Innovation data on production practices and residue profiles for specific vegetable and citrus categories informs context in the Tread Careful Ten guide, particularly for snow peas and domestic growing practices.
